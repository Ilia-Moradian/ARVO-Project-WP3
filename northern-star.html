Who it is for

One site, four kinds of readers

The content is organised around the questions each group asks, not around the project's work packages.

Municipalities and regional decision-makers

Plan siting, land use and procurement so that more value stays local.

Business development organisations

Guide companies and investors towards local partners.

SMEs and growth firms

Understand what large projects need and how to become a supplier.

Investors and EPC contractors

Find local capacity and understand the regional context.
