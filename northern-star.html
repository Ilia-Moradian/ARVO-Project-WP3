Why it matters

Large projects bring investment to the region. Whether the value stays depends on who does the work.

Wind, solar and hydrogen projects are usually built by large contractors with global supply chains. A small local firm can rarely meet the volume, delivery time and paperwork alone.

Together, local firms can do more. ARVO studies how to make that possible, and Northern Star shares what we learn.

Learning from the Nordics

Norway and Denmark have long experience in turning large energy projects into work for local firms. ARVO compares them, with Sweden as supporting evidence, and asks what Northern Ostrobothnia can learn.

About

The ARVO project

ARVO (Value added from sustainable energy business) studies how large green-transition investments can create more value, jobs and new business in Northern Ostrobothnia. The project is carried out by the Kerttu Saalasti Institute, University of Oulu.
